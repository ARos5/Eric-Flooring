## TL;DR — What Eric wants

A **quarterly (maybe monthly) email newsletter** for his flooring business, **LVP Jacksonville**, that:

1. **Reports Northeast Florida real estate market trends** — interest rates, property values, overall inventory, and (ideally) **off-market / wholesale ("off-MLS") activity**, because the people buying those houses are the ones remodeling and buying flooring.
2. **Showcases flooring designs** and promotes **new arrivals and sales**.
3. Goes out **automatically on a set cadence**, pulling its audience from his **QuickBooks customer list**.

The real audience is **real estate investors / flippers**, plus **repeat buyers** and **general contractors**. Goal: stay top-of-mind so that when an investor closes a flip, LVP Jacksonville is who they call for flooring.
