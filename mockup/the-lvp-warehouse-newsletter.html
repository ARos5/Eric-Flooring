What this means for flippers

Inventory is down ~15% year-over-year and homes are still moving — but with rates near 6.5%, buyers are picky and rewarding finished quality, not just the lowest price. Translation: the renovated-to-sell projects win, and flooring is the first thing a buyer notices walking in. Lock your materials before your next close so the install doesn't hold up your timeline.
